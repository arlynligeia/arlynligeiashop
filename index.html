UNIVERSE

Ladies and Gentlemen,

Welcome to my Universe...

This is a space where the cosmic, the technological, and the vaporwave coexist; where past and future merge into the present; where emotion and reason unite into a single feeling; and where art and science intertwine with a shared purpose, the expression and curiosity that arise from within me to manifest as art.

My work moves through transhumanism, tracing the edge between the human and the artificial, the biological and the designed, the mortal and what comes after, approached without judgment, only precision. That same curiosity draws me toward the sciences that inhabit my art; psychology, physics, and biology, three disciplines through which I explore the depths of the human mind, the immensity of space and time, and the evolution of life, from what we have been to what we are becoming.

To me, art is a language of the soul, born from within and moving outward.

Every project, whether a film, a song, a garment, an accessory, a piece of jewelry, a painting, or a verse, is born from a need to explore, feel, and transmit.

Here you will find my works, as well as fragments of my thoughts, my obsessions, my questions, and my transformations.

Arlyn Ligeia
08/01/2025
